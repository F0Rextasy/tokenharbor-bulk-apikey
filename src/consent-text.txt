Token Harbor may keep the requests and responses you send through free models and use them for service diagnostics, safety, optimization, and to evaluate and improve models and products. Your consent also covers free models we add later. Our staff may read free-route conversations and may use third-party AI tools to help analyze them, and the providers that serve free models may use them under their own terms, including to train their own models. Paid routes are never used for training and are not stored, apart from a cache of up to five minutes and short excerpts kept when an automated safety check flags a request.
